About West Auckland Windscreens

We're West Auckland windscreen specialists. Honest advice, quality workmanship and a fully mobile service across West Auckland.

When most people search for "windscreen repair", they're actually dealing with damage that needs full replacement. We've seen too many vehicle owners get a chip repair that should have been a replacement — leaving them back at square one.

That's why we don't offer chip or crack repair. We focus on reinstating your vehicle to its original, pre-damage state: full glass replacement, done properly, with quality glass and professional fitting. If you've got a small repairable chip, we'll point you to the chip-repair specialists on 0800 202 888.

We specialise in vehicle glass because it's different — and owners who can't afford long downtime. We're set up for exactly that.

And we're mobile — we come to you. No waiting rooms, no dropping the vehicle off. We work around your schedule, anywhere across Auckland.
