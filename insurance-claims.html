How Windscreen Insurance Claims Work

In New Zealand, most comprehensive vehicle insurance policies include windscreen and glass cover. That means if your windscreen, side window or rear glass is damaged, your insurer will cover the replacement cost — usually minus a small glass excess (often $0 to $100).

At West Auckland Windscreens, the majority of our jobs are insurance claims. We make the process simple: you call us, we handle the rest.

Tip: You don't need to call your insurer first. In many cases, the glass company can lodge the claim on your behalf. Call us and we'll tell you the fastest path.

Insurers We Work With

We invoice and deal directly with all major New Zealand insurers, including:

  • AA Insurance — One of the largest providers in NZ. Glass claims are usually handled through their approved supplier network.
  • State Insurance — Comprehensive policies typically include windscreen cover with a low excess.
  • AMI Insurance — Glass cover is standard on most comprehensive policies.
  • Vero — Business and private vehicle policies; we invoice Vero directly.
  • FMG — Rural and commercial vehicle specialists; we handle FMG claims regularly.
  • Tower Insurance — Glass cover included on comprehensive plans.
  • And more — If your insurer isn't listed, call us. We can work with almost any NZ insurer.

The 4-Step Claim Process

1

Check Your Policy

Confirm you have glass cover and note your excess amount. This is usually shown in your policy document or app.

2

Call Us First

Phone 09 827-2827 with your vehicle make, model, year, registration, policy number and insurer name.

3
